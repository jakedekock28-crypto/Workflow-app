## Using a workspace

Workflow helps a workspace organise and assign tasks. The workspace Owner controls membership, roles, task visibility, and workspace deletion. Owners and Admins can view all workspace tasks. Delegators can manage tasks they create. Members can view and update tasks assigned to their linked person record. The Owner can enable broader task visibility for Members.

## Information you may enter

Only enter names, email addresses, short role descriptions, and task information needed to coordinate work. Do not enter details about children or minors, sensitive personal or health information, service or identity numbers, credentials, security plans, or sensitive operational details. Do not use an invite link for anyone other than the invited email address.

## Email and notifications

Workflow does not automatically send invitation or reminder emails. A **Send reminder** action opens a draft in the user’s email app. The user must review and send it. A “sent” entry is recorded only after that user confirms they sent it; Workflow cannot verify delivery. Browser notifications depend on device/browser support and permission.

## Accounts and security

Sign-in uses Google Identity Services. Members must protect their Google account and device. Workspace Owners and Admins should assign the least access needed and remove members who no longer need access. Sessions expire and removed members lose workspace access on their next API request.

## Data, backups, and availability

Cloud records are stored in Cloudflare D1 and are subject to provider availability and free-tier limits. The app is designed to stop or show errors when those limits are reached; it will not upgrade itself to a paid plan. Keep exports and backups in a secure place. Workspace edits and deletions sync and are not a substitute for a backup.

## Removal and deletion

Members can leave a workspace. Owners and Admins can remove members. The Owner can delete a workspace and all its stored records. Account deletion may require the Owner to transfer ownership or remove members first. Downloaded backups remain under the control of whoever saved them and must be deleted separately.
