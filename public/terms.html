Using a workspace

Workflow helps a workspace organise and assign tasks. The Owner controls membership, roles, visibility, and workspace deletion. Owners and Admins can see all tasks. Delegators can manage tasks they create. Members can see and update tasks assigned to their linked contact. The Owner can allow broader task visibility.

Information and acceptable use

Enter only names, email addresses, optional short role descriptions, and task information needed to coordinate work. Do not enter information about children or minors, sensitive personal or health information, identity/service numbers, credentials, security plans, or sensitive operational details. Share invite links only with the intended invitee.

Email reminders and availability

Workflow does not automatically send invitation or reminder emails. A reminder opens a draft that the user must review and send. The user may record that they sent it; Workflow cannot verify delivery. Browser alerts depend on device support and run only while Workflow is open. Cloud records depend on Cloudflare availability and free-tier limits. The app will not automatically upgrade to a paid plan.

Security, backup, and deletion

Sign-in uses Google Identity Services. Members should protect their Google account and device. Keep secure backups; cloud sync also syncs edits and deletions. Workspace deletion erases the workspace’s cloud records. Downloaded files and local browser copies must be deleted separately. Account deletion may require ownership transfer or removal of workspace members first.
