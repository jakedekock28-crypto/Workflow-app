Information collected

Google sign-in provides Workflow with your Google account identifier and verified email address. Workflow does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, or contacts. Workspace data includes names, email addresses, optional short roles, workspace names and settings, membership roles, groups, task details, task templates, invitation addresses/status, and short-lived audit records. Session tokens are stored as hashes. Sign-in and invite rate-limit identifiers are hashed.

Do not enter details about children or minors, sensitive personal or health information, identity or service numbers, credentials, security plans, or sensitive operational information. Workflow has no people notes, phone numbers, SMS data, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.

Storage and access

Local app data is stored in this browser. If cloud workspaces are enabled, account and workspace records are processed by Cloudflare Workers and stored in Cloudflare D1. The database may use an Oceania location hint, which does not guarantee Australia-only processing or storage. Google processes sign-in identity. GitHub Pages serves the app and may collect visitor information under GitHub’s terms.

Owners and Admins can see all workspace tasks. Delegators can see tasks they created or are assigned to. Members see their assigned tasks by default; an Owner can enable broader visibility. Task text is not sent to Google.

Reminders

Workflow does not send email automatically. A reminder opens an email draft for the user to review and send. A local reminder history entry is stored only after the user confirms sending. Reminder history is removed after 90 days by default and can be configured from 30 to 365 days. Browser alerts use generic text, remain on the device, and work only while Workflow is open.

Retention and deletion

Audit records expire after 90 days by default; the workspace Owner or Admin can configure 30–365 days. Rate-limit counters expire after 30 days. Owners can export or delete a workspace and request deletion of completed tasks. Account deletion and member removal are available in workspace settings, subject to ownership transfer requirements. Downloaded backups and local browser data must be deleted separately.

Australian privacy matters

The workspace Owner should check whether the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and Australian Privacy Principles apply and consider APP 8 obligations for overseas recipients. See OAIC APP 8 guidance. This template is not legal advice. Ask [Privacy contact] about access, correction, or deletion requests.

Terms

Read the Workflow terms. Review this notice whenever providers, data collected, or workspace access changes.
